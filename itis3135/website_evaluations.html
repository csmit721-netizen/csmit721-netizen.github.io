Website Evaluations

The CRAP principles are Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity. They help explain what makes a website easy and visually appealing.

AccuWeather

  • Contrast: The different colors help separate information, but the number of elements competing for attention can make the page feel cluttered.
  • Repetition: Weather information and navigation options repeat throughout the page. This creates consistency, but there is a lot to look at.
  • Alignment: The page uses organized sections, but the different content areas can make it harder to focus on the forecast.
  • Proximity: Related weather details are grouped together, but advertisements and other content can distract from the forecast.

LEGO

  • Contrast: LEGO uses bright colors, especially its recognizable red branding, to make important buttons and products stand out.
  • Repetition: Product cards, images, buttons, and headings follow a consistent style throughout the shopping experience.
  • Alignment: Producrs are arranged in organized tows and sections, making the page easier to browse.
  • Proximity: Product images, names, prices, and shopping buttons appear close together, making it easy to find information about each set.
